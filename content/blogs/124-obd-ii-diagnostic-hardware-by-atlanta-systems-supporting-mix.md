## Technical Questions Answered by Our Engineers

### Q1: Will installing Atlanta telematics affect my vehicle manufacturer warranty?
No. Our systems use contactless magnetic induction clamps over the twisted CAN-High and CAN-Low wires. Because there is zero wire splicing or electrical contact made to the copper conductor, factory OEM warranties from Volvo, Scania, Mercedes-Benz, Freightliner, and Caterpillar remain fully valid.

### Q2: Can the device cause interference or inject packets on the vehicle CAN network?
No. The reader functions purely as a passive listener with zero transmitting circuitry connected to the vehicle bus. It is physically impossible for the hardware to inject rogue packets or interfere with electronic braking, steering, or transmission controls.

### Q3: Does it support mixed fleets with light-duty vehicles?
Yes. The system automatically auto-detects baud rates and decodes commercial SAE J1939 and J1708 protocols as well as passenger and light-duty commercial OBD-II (ISO 15765-4 CAN) protocols.
