## Technical Questions Answered by Our Engineers

### Q1: Does the DMS camera work if the driver is wearing dark sunglasses?
Yes. 940nm near-infrared light passes directly through tinted and polarized sunglasses, allowing the NPU to track iris and eyelid movements accurately.

### Q2: Does the camera transmit continuous in-cab video of the driver to dispatch?
No. To protect driver privacy and comply with labor privacy laws, the camera processes video entirely at the edge, transmitting video clips only when a verified fatigue or mobile phone infraction occurs.

### Q3: How does the system handle bumpy roads and driver head movements?
Our algorithm incorporates dynamic 3D head pose estimation, distinguishing normal mirror checks from drowsy head nodding.
