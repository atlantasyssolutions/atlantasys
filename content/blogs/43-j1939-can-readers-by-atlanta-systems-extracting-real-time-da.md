## Technical Questions Answered by Our Engineers

### Q1: Will installing the EC-400 void my vehicle manufacturer warranty?
No. The EC-400 uses contactless magnetic induction clamps. Because wire insulation remains completely untouched with zero electrical splicing, OEM warranties remain fully valid.

### Q2: Can the EC-400 cause interference on the vehicle CAN network?
No. The EC-400 is a purely passive listener. It has no transmitting circuitry connected to the vehicle bus, making it physically impossible to inject rogue packets or interfere with vehicle braking and steering controls.

### Q3: Does it support mixed fleets with light-duty vehicles?
Yes. The system automatically auto-detects baud rates and decodes commercial SAE J1939 and J1708 protocols as well as passenger and light-duty OBD-II (ISO 15765-4 CAN) protocols.
